# Ecommerce Sales & Customer Analytics —  Dataset

for a Sales, Revenue and Customer Analytics portfolio project.

## Size
- Customers: 12,000
- Products: 180
- Orders: 70,000
- Product-month records: 5,940
- Order period: 2024-01-01 to 2026-09-01

## Tables
1. customers.csv — customer profile, acquisition economics and lifecycle metrics
2. products.csv — product/category/pricing/cost/brand information
3. orders.csv — transactional fact table
4. campaigns.csv — campaign and budget information
5. product_monthly_snapshot.csv — product-month trend/seasonality table
6. data_dictionary.csv — definitions

## Designed analytical patterns
The data contains:
- seasonal peaks and troughs
- a deliberate revenue slowdown period and later recovery
- categories with different growth trajectories
- high-revenue/low-margin product archetypes
- high-margin/low-volume product archetypes
- discount-heavy customer behavior
- new vs returning customers
- repeat-purchase timing
- inactive and churn-risk customers
- different acquisition costs
- customer lifetime value proxy
- geography differences
- customer price-sensitivity bands
- campaign attribution
- returns/refunds and contribution-profit effects
