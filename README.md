E-Commerce Sales, Customer & Marketing Analytics

An end-to-end Data Analytics portfolio project focused on understanding e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, retention, profitability, and marketing efficiency.

The project follows a practical analytics workflow using Excel, MySQL, Python, and Power BI, transforming raw transactional and customer data into business-focused insights and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

📌 Project Overview

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional, customer, product, and marketing data. The objective of this project is to analyze that data across multiple business areas and identify patterns that can support better decisions around:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Product and category performance
- Profitability
- Customer acquisition and value
- Customer retention and churn
- Campaign performance
- Geographic customer behavior
- Sales seasonality
- Discounts and profitability

The project covers a set of interconnected business questions rather than treating each question as a separate analysis.

---

🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering questions such as:

Sales & Revenue

- How is revenue performing over time?
- Which categories and products contribute most to revenue?
- Which products generate high revenue relative to profit?
- Which products generate higher profit with comparatively lower sales?
- What are the monthly sales patterns and peak periods?
- What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
- How does discounting relate to profitability?
- Which categories are growing or declining over time?

Customers & Retention

- How are customers distributed geographically?
- What proportion of customers are new vs returning?
- What is the repeat purchase rate?
- How many customers can be identified as churned based on purchase inactivity?
- Who are the high-value customers?
- What is the overall Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)?

Marketing & Customer Value

- Which campaigns generate the most revenue?
- How does Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) compare with Customer Lifetime Value?
- How are customers distributed across value segments?

- Customer distribution by city
- New vs returning customers

- High-value customer identification

🧮 Key DAX Measures

Examples of the measures used in the Power BI model include:

Total Revenue =
SUM(Orders[Realized_revenue])

Total Orders =
DISTINCTCOUNT(Orders[Order_ID])

Total Customers =
DISTINCTCOUNT(Customers[Customer_ID])

AOV =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Revenue],
    [Total Orders]
)

Total Profit =
SUMX(
    Orders,
    Orders[Realized_revenue]
        - Orders[Quantity] * RELATED(Products[Unit_cost])
)

Discount Rate =
DIVIDE(
    SUM(Orders[Discount_Amount]),
    SUM(Orders[Realized_revenue])
        + SUM(Orders[Discount_Amount]),
    0
)
